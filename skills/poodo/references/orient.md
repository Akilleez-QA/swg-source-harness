# Orient operation

Build and challenge explanations of the evidence. Useful operations include competing hypotheses, causal graphs, fault/change analysis, feedback modeling, and constraint analysis.

Preserve the strongest credible rival and disconfirming evidence. Decompose confidence across artifact identity, test-path validity, causal model, and outcome status; the weakest load-bearing dimension caps the conclusion. Guard against hindsight, monocausal stories, chronology-as-causation, and correlated evidence families masquerading as corroboration.

Before enumerating paths or converging, apply [semantic-topology.md](semantic-topology.md). Use parallel fan-out to traverse separable regions when it improves coverage. Do not collapse merely because one coherent explanation appeared early.

## Mandatory 20 paths forward

Before convergence, enumerate exactly 20 named and materially distinct options, solutions, experiments, or paths forward. Each entry must state its distinguishing mechanism or information gain in one compact line. Generate from the observed evidence and completed research record rather than padding a predetermined answer.

Make the option space genuinely broad. Where applicable include minimal correction, exact known-good replication, differential experiment, instrumentation, rollback, containment, alternate architecture, dependency substitution, independent reproduction, staged rollout, deferral pending evidence, stop/no-action, and escalation to a domain authority. These are prompts for diversity, not a fixed quota by category.

Apply these validity rules:

- Two paths are distinct only if they materially differ in mechanism, evidence gained, risk, reversibility, cost, or authority boundary.
- Parameter tweaks, renamed duplicates, and arbitrary decomposition of one action do not count as separate paths.
- Do not make weak paths appear equally credible. Mark dominated, unsafe, unauthorized, or evidence-incompatible options plainly after all 20 have been surfaced.
- An experiment or information-gathering path counts when it could discriminate live hypotheses or change the decision.
- A stop, defer, rollback, or escalate path counts when it represents a real decision boundary, not filler.
- Generating 20 paths does not authorize any of them and does not weaken precommitment, evidence, safety, or user-scope requirements.

After the list is complete, cluster related paths, identify eliminations with reasons, preserve the strongest credible alternatives, and carry the viable set into Decide. The mandatory divergence is reasoning substrate; expose the full list to the user when requested or decision-relevant, but do not force twenty-item verbosity into every external response.
