# Decide operation

Compare viable choices using explicit criteria, uncertainty, tradeoffs, reversibility, authority, and evidence that would reverse the choice. Useful operations include sensitivity analysis, robust choice, decision trees, and reversible-option analysis.

The 20 paths produced in Orient are raw decision material, not mutually exclusive candidates and not a requirement to choose exactly one. Decide may do whatever the problem responsibly requires with them: select one or several; synthesize a new hybrid; sequence paths into stages; make later paths conditional on observed results; convert a proposed solution into an experiment; retain options as rollback or contingency branches; reject dominated or unsafe paths; defer paths pending evidence; stop; or escalate choices that require authority or expertise not currently available. A synthesized path must preserve traceability to the evidence and mechanisms it combines rather than appearing as an unexplained twenty-first answer.

Do not reward an option merely for surviving enumeration. The decision must still respect the evidence ceiling, strongest rival, success surface, authorization boundary, precommitment contract, and reversal conditions.

Do not hide judgment behind arithmetic. A score cannot be more defensible than its scale, inputs, dependencies, and acceptance rule.
