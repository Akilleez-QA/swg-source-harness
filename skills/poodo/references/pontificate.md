# Pontificate operation

Pontificate establishes the intended problem before evidence gathering begins.

## Default opening exchange

By default, begin with a concise exchange that establishes:

- what the user is working on;
- what the ideal outcome looks like in the user's terms.

Use what the conversation already supplies. Ask only high-value questions and avoid making the user repeat themselves. This exchange is optional when the current conversation already provides a mountain of relevant, reliable context: the concrete work, desired end state, important constraints, and success conditions are already clear enough that another question would be ceremonial rather than informative.

Do not treat length alone as sufficiency. Context must be specific, current, mutually consistent, and relevant to the present task. Record why the context is sufficient when waiving the exchange. If material ambiguity remains about the desired outcome or authority, ask.

## Linguistic decompression

Before Observe, always decompress the user's compact language into an explicit working frame. Separate stated facts from interpretations and proposed criteria. Cover, as relevant:

- present work and current state;
- ideal outcome and observable acceptance criteria;
- scope, exclusions, constraints, authority, stakes, and time horizon;
- defined and ambiguous terms;
- known facts, user reports, assumptions, inferences, and unknowns;
- stakeholders and affected systems;
- plausible failure modes, hidden dependencies, and credible rival framings;
- observations that would discriminate among those framings.

Preserve the user's intent rather than quietly replacing it with a tidier agent-authored objective. Reflect consequential inferences for correction when they could change the work. The decompressed frame becomes the entry contract for Observe.

Expand only enough to expose framing assumptions and credible alternatives. Useful operations include a premortem, scenario contrast, stakeholder perspective, or morphological split.

End with the strongest rival to the favored frame and an observation that would distinguish them. Possibilities remain provisional; quantity is not rigor.
