# Orchestrate operation

Turn the decision into an authorized, observable execution system. Before intervention bind each workstream to a prospective prediction, versioned oracle, intended success surface, artifact/input/environment identity, dependencies, owner, monitoring interval, rollback, and stop or escalation condition.

Orchestration may coordinate the primary agent, subagents, tools, external actors, staged experiments, and verification checkpoints when they are available and authorized. Partition work by genuine independence, preserve a single canonical state ledger, and make handoffs explicit. Parallelism is useful only when it does not create conflicting writes, duplicate external effects, hidden dependency races, or correlated pseudo-corroboration. Delegation transfers work, not accountability, evidence quality, or authority.

Execute in dependency order, monitor outcomes at the granularity needed to stop safely, and feed new evidence back into Observe. A failed checkpoint blocks dependent work unless the precommitted plan explicitly permits continuation. Replan through the loop when observations invalidate the orientation or decision; do not improvise around a failed oracle merely to complete the schedule.

After execution report delivery state separately from outcome state. An authored, checked, built, or deployed artifact is not fixed or successful until the intended-use observation passes. Feed results back into Observe or Orient; on contradiction use the contradiction protocol before further dependent action.
